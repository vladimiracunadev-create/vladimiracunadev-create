## 🛰️ NASA Space Apps 2026 hackathon

I took part in the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** (Santiago, November 14–15), on the *Planet X and SPHEREx* challenge, in a team of 6. The product is **AstroTrack · SPHEREx**: a web viewer where you type sky coordinates and see that region as a sequence of SPHEREx images over time, using NASA/IPAC IRSA data.

- **My part:** data access (IRSA queries with caching and precomputed demo fields), packaging (PWA, Tauri desktop installers and a Capacitor Android APK), the “Downloads” view, and a consistency audit between documentation and code.
- **Verifiable delivery:** [spherex-releases](https://github.com/vladimiracunadev-create/spherex-releases) gathers 7 releases (v0.1.0 to v0.4.2) with SHA-256 checksums · [web app](https://spherex-planet-x-tau.vercel.app/) · [team repository](https://github.com/mauriciovalenzuela742/SPHEREX).
- **Declared limits:** the viewer, the design and the rest of the backend are the work of other team members; the installers are unsigned and were not tested on real devices; I used AI (Claude Code) as an assistant, as declared in the repository. This describes my contribution, not a hackathon result.

## 🧠 Standards I Apply Across Repositories

- **DX first** — if it is not easy to run, it is not useful as a demo or as a team base.
- **Observability from day 1** — metrics and traceability, not an afterthought.
- **Security as pipeline** — secret scanning, SBOM per release, explicitly forbidden practices.
- **Real resilience** — idempotency, circuit breaker, and controlled degradation.
- **Polyglot by design** — language and engine chosen by use case, never by fashion.
- **Honesty about status** — one repo was renamed (`Multisimulador` → `machine-operator-program`) to avoid promising software that does not exist yet.
- **Badges are signals, not evidence** — each repo also declares **what it does NOT do**.
- **Model outward** — the domain is designed first; the interface is a projection.
- **External work is declared as external** — the [60 forks](docs/ECOSYSTEM.en.md#forks) of third-party projects are described one by one and stay out of every own-work figure in this profile.
