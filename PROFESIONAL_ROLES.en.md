This matrix widens the professional field without turning education, personal projects or documented knowledge into unproven work experience. The 16+ years of technical experience and the public repositories support the technology base; formal responsibility for people, budget, sales, contracts, audits or corporate policies is considered proven only when there is specific evidence.

## How to read the classification

| Code | Category | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **EA** | Proven experience | The profile already declares and backs the performance of the role with a track record and public technical evidence. |
| **CT** | Transferable skill | There are directly reusable capabilities and artifacts, but it is not claimed that the role has been held. |
| **PP** | Application possibility | The dual education and part of the evidence allow competing if the scope of the opening matches and the gaps are not disqualifying. |
| **OD** | Future development goal | Requires formal experience, results or additional credentials before it is presented as a direct fit. |

**Programming:** `None` = not a core function; `Occasional` = help with validation, automation or technical conversation; `Regular` = a normal part of the role.

**Market experience:** indicative range observed for the level, not a universal rule nor experience attributed to Vladimir. In Chile the titles «encargado», «coordinador», «jefe» or «subgerente» may appear where the international market uses `lead`, `head` or `manager`.

## Recommended prioritization

### Immediate application with an evidence-based narrative

1. Solutions Consultant / technical pre-sales.
2. Business Analyst, functional or process analyst.
3. IT Project Coordinator / PMO Analyst.
4. Product Operations / technical Product Owner depending on scope.
5. Digital transformation and legacy modernization consultant.
6. Technology instructor, EdTech or instructional design.
7. Quality analyst, continuous improvement or technology risk.
8. Data Steward, data governance analyst or AI adoption/governance.

### Application conditional on the scope of the opening

- Project Manager, Service Delivery, IT Operations, GRC, technical Customer Success, administration/operations, management control and public ICT coordination.
- Before applying, adapt the CV with a verifiable case showing context, personal responsibility, decision, result and limits.

### Future development

- IT Manager, CIO, operations manager, digital transformation manager, controller and high-responsibility public leadership roles.
- The critical step is not adding titles to the profile: it is producing verifiable work evidence of leading people, budget, vendors, risk and results.

## Cross-cutting gaps and recommendations

1. **Organizational responsibility:** document team size, budget, vendors, SLA, customers and results when legitimate evidence exists.
2. **Non-technical cases:** prepare 4–6 STAR/CAR cases on requirements, prioritization, negotiation, continuity, improvement and adoption; do not present repositories alone as business experience.
3. **Certifications by goal, not by accumulation:** CAPM/PMI-PBA or ECBA for formal entry; PMP/CCBA/CBAP only if requirements are met; ITIL 4 for service; CISA/CISM/CRISC or ISO 27001 for GRC; ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF for AI governance.
4. **Quantified results:** convert evidence into metrics of time, quality, risk, availability, adoption, cost or revenue when they can be verified.
5. **Chilean market:** strengthen ChileCompra, contracts, labor/tax regulation and Law 21,180 for administration and the public sector.
6. **Languages and international narrative:** use the equivalents `Business Analyst`, `Solutions Consultant`, `Product Operations`, `Service Delivery`, `Data Steward` and `AI Governance` without translating them literally when the market recognizes them in English.
