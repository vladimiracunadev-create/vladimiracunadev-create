## How I work

1. **Diagnosis before intervention:** I understand the system and its risks before proposing changes.
2. **Reproducible delivery:** what is delivered can be cloned, run and validated.
3. **Visible quality:** tests, CI and security checks are part of the delivery.
4. **Documentation by audience:** whoever operates, decides or develops finds their part.
5. **Honest status:** every piece is declared operative, documented or planned.

## To get a quote, send me a short brief

- Business goal and what success means.
- Technical context: stack, repositories, environment and access to review.
- Constraints: deadlines, reference budget, available team, compliance.
- What was already tried and what failed.

## Limits

- The profile's projects are my own and lab work; they are not client cases.
- If something falls outside what I can stand behind, I say so before starting.
- Mode: remote or hybrid, depending on the project.
